Privacy Policy

Kaizen JLPT for Android. Last updated: 23 November 2026

Summary

Kaizen JLPT does not collect, store or share any personal information. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts and never asks for your name, email address, location, contacts or any other personal information.

Data stored on your device

Your study progress (which words you have studied, your review schedule, streak, quiz results and starred words) and your settings are saved on your device only. This data is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your device settings, permanently deletes this data. You can also erase it with "Reset progress" inside the app.

Advertising and analytics

The app shows no advertisements and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software.

Purchases

The app offers an optional one-time purchase, Kaizen Pro. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your card details, name or address; the app only learns from Google Play whether your Google account owns Kaizen Pro. Google's handling of your payment is covered by Google's own privacy policy.

Sharing and backups

The app shares something only when you tap a share or export button. Your result, or a backup file of your progress, is then passed to the app you choose in your device's share menu. What that app does with it is covered by that app's own privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Vibration: for a short buzz when you answer.
  • Network access and billing: used only to talk to Google Play about the optional Kaizen Pro purchase. Studying works fully offline and no study data is sent anywhere.

Words are read aloud by the text-to-speech engine already installed on your device.

Children

The app is intended for general audiences aged 13 and over and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
